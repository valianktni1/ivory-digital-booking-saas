1. PACKAGES & PRICING

[ ] Open Packages & pricing.
[ ] Confirm package cards are large, readable and show wording, deposit, order,
    balance timing, visibility and price.

    package name, full price, booking fee/deposit, display position, visibility,
    balance due days, featured choice, short summary, full package wording and
    optional information URL.
[ ] Save several paragraphs/bullet lines in Full package wording and reopen the
    editor to prove they were stored.

[ ] Create a compulsory add-on and enter the reason it is compulsory.
[ ] Create a reusable private discount.
[ ] Confirm the private discount is clearly identified and is not treated as a

[ ] Include the saved private discount.
[ ] Save the quote as a draft and reopen it.
[ ] Confirm packages, add-ons, compulsory choices, discount and message remain.
[ ] Review the email and send the quote.
