PHASE 5.6.5 - FINAL TIMINGS CHECKLIST

1. Accept a test quote.
2. Refresh the private client area.
3. Confirm the Booking Questionnaire is visible.
4. Confirm Final Wedding Timings is not visible.
5. In Emails & workflow, create or edit a wedding-date email.
6. Choose 60 days or 30 days before the wedding.
7. Tick Include the Final Timings form and choose review or automatic sending.
8. After the test email has actually sent, open its secure button.
9. Confirm Final Wedding Timings is now visible for that couple.
10. Confirm other couples whose timing email has not sent still cannot see it.
