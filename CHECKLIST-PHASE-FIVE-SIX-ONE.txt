PHASE 5.6.1 COMPLETE DUMMY JOURNEY

Before starting

[ ] Open Business & brand and upload a temporary or real studio logo.
[ ] Confirm the logo preview appears on that same page.
[ ] Open Email templates and confirm the six editable starter templates appear.
[ ] Open the Wedding quote template, make a harmless wording change, save it,
    reopen it and confirm the change remains.
[ ] Open Contracts & forms and save the agreement wording that should be used.
[ ] Confirm outgoing email is connected and verified.

Enquiry and quote

[ ] Submit a new dummy enquiry using an email inbox you can access.
[ ] Open Enquiries. Open the dummy enquiry and confirm it stays in Enquiries.
[ ] Confirm it does not appear under Weddings yet.
[ ] In the quote, offer at least one package.
[ ] Include at least two extras. Set one Optional and one Compulsory.
[ ] Save the draft and confirm no email is sent.
[ ] Choose Save & review email.
[ ] Check the recipient, subject, editable message and large quote-button preview.
[ ] Send the quote once.
[ ] Open the received email and confirm the logo, wording and large button appear.

Couple choice and promotion

[ ] Open the secure quote using the button.
[ ] Confirm no package is selected automatically.
[ ] Choose a package and confirm only compatible extras appear.
[ ] Confirm the compulsory extra is locked on and the optional extra can be
    selected or left out.
[ ] Accept the quote using the dummy couple's name.
[ ] Return to Studio. Confirm the enquiry now says Booked.
[ ] Open Weddings and confirm the couple now appears there with one invoice.
[ ] Reopen the private link and confirm the accepted package, extras, adjustments
    and total are itemised.

Forms and agreement

[ ] Confirm the Booking Questionnaire and Final Wedding Timings forms are now
    available in the private link.
[ ] Submit a dummy Booking Questionnaire and confirm it appears in Studio.
[ ] Confirm the active agreement is already available without manually issuing it.
[ ] Sign the agreement once in the private link.
[ ] Confirm the private page now says Agreement complete and shows both names.
[ ] Download the agreement PDF and confirm both signatures are printed.
[ ] Open the confirmation email and confirm it says both parties have signed.
[ ] Open its PDF attachment and confirm it is the same completed agreement.
[ ] In Studio Communications/email history, open that sent message and download
    the stored PDF attachment again.

Do not use a real couple until every required check above passes.
